# PageRankCollegeFootball
Tests using Page Rank to Create the College Football Rankings.

Motivation: 

The college football rankings are controversial every year. There are accusations of name bias or conference bias. Strength of schedule and record are important to ranking teams, but the CFP committee has often justified their rankings on live television with buzzwords such as "the eye test." This method aims to reward teams for winning their games and playing tougher opponents. 

Assumptions:
1. Home/Away will not used as a metric. If a team can't handle a hostile road environment, they won't be able to handle a the pressure of a playoff environment. 
2. Injuries will not be used as a metric. Depth is important to the makeup of the team.
3. Crucial calls by the referees will not be accounted for. The refs aren't rigging the game put the beer down.
4. Later wins count the same as earlier win. A win against any give team counts the same whether its in week 1 or week 13.
5. Rankings will be determined one season at a time. Everyone starts at ground zero in week 0. 
6. FCS wins will not count. Cry about it.
7. Score differential will not be used as a metric. 

Key Questions:
1. Will one conference dominate the rankings? Expect: No. There should be a mix of conferences high in the rankings.
2. Will group of 5/6 show up high in the rankings? Expect: No. Other than one or two outliers (Ex. Boise State), the power 4 should dominate the top of the rankings.
3. Will teams like 2025 Notre Dame, 2024 Alabama, 2023 Florida State, 2022 TCU, ... make it to the playoff. Expect: Unsure. Don't want opinions on this to affect the quality of solution.
